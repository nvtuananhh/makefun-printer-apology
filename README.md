One-line apology from an office printer to the people who keep jamming it, feeding it cheap paper and swearing at it — written for the [The Printer Apology](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-the-printer-apology-60229153) bounty on make.fun.

## The entry

> Sorry I jammed — after 10,000 pages of cheap paper, three staples and zero thank-yous, I printed my resignation.

The printer apologises, lists its real grievances, and ends with a single twist: it quits, by printing its own resignation.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt` and nothing else.
